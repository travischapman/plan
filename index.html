Progress Rules

Expected pace: 0.3-1.0 lb/week. Use weekly averages, waist measurements, progress photos, and training performance to decide whether the plan is working.

Every Sunday: compare the weekly average weight, waist, photos, strength, and energy.

If nothing moves for 2 weeks: remove 100 calories or add 2,000 steps/day.

If loss is too fast or workouts suffer: add 100-150 calories, especially carbs around training.

Reference

Training details

Strength: 3 sets of 8-12 reps for most lifts. Stop with 1-3 reps left. Add weight when every set reaches the top of the range cleanly.

Core: 3-4 days/week for 8-12 minutes. Use cable crunches, Pallof presses, dead bugs, side planks, hanging knee raises, or reverse crunches.

Recovery: if sleep or stress is rough, keep the workout but remove one set per exercise.

Why these numbers

The starting target of about 1,600 calories is designed as a moderate deficit for an active adult while leaving room to train, recover, and preserve lean mass.

Protein is set at 125-135g/day to support lean-mass retention during fat loss. The goal is not simply to get lighter; it is to get leaner while keeping or building muscle.

Health note

This is general nutrition and fitness guidance, not medical care. Check with a clinician before starting a fat-loss phase if pregnant, breastfeeding, managing a history of disordered eating, dealing with metabolic or hormonal conditions, or taking medications affected by diet or exercise.
